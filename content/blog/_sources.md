# Vetted sources

Claims a post may cite, each checked against its source. The drafting job cites from this list first. A new source is allowed only if the drafter fetched the page itself and quotes what it says; add it here in the same format when you do.

Solidity: **solid** (well-replicated), **contested** (real but disputed), **thin** (small or correlational), **folklore** (do not cite as fact).

## Lectures and recorded lectures

- **Active learning beats lecturing in STEM.** 225 comparisons: exam scores +0.47 SD; students in traditional lectures 1.5x more likely to fail (odds ratio 1.95). Caveat: "active learning" is a grab-bag of methods. *Solid.* Freeman et al. 2014, PNAS. https://www.pnas.org/doi/abs/10.1073/pnas.1319030111
- **A fluent lecture feels like learning but isn't.** Students rated a fluent instructor far higher and predicted they learned more; test scores were identical to a disfluent version. *Solid.* Carpenter et al. 2018, Metacognition and Learning. https://link.springer.com/article/10.1007/s11409-017-9175-0
- **Students learn more in active classes but feel they learned less,** and rate passive lectures higher. Randomized, large intro physics. *Solid.* Deslauriers et al. 2019, PNAS. https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/31484770/
- **Questions inside a lecture video cut mind-wandering from 41% to 19%,** tripled note-taking, lifted final-segment scores from 70% to 89%. A re-watch control did no better than nothing: retrieval did the work, re-exposure didn't. n=80, effect size likely optimistic, direction solid. Szpunar, Khan & Schacter 2013, PNAS. https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC3631699/
- **"Attention drops after 10-15 minutes" is folklore.** A review found little support for it. *Folklore; cite only to debunk.* Wilson & Korn 2007, Teaching of Psychology. https://journals.sagepub.com/doi/10.1080/00986280701291291
- **Median engagement with edX videos is ~6 minutes regardless of video length.** 6.9M sessions. Measures watch time, not learning. *Solid as description, not causal.* Guo, Kim & Rubin 2014. https://dl.acm.org/doi/10.1145/2556325.2566239
- **Lecture capture lowered attendance, and attendance predicted grades.** Viewing recordings didn't compensate. Other studies disagree. *Contested.* Edwards & Clinton 2019, Higher Education. https://link.springer.com/article/10.1007/s10734-018-0275-9
- **Over half of recording views happen within 24 hours of an exam** (cramming, not spaced study). https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC12667208/

## Online courses

- **MOOC certification fell to 3.13% by 2017-18; ~52% of registrants never open the course.** 12.67M registrations. Do not generalize to paid courses. *Solid for open MOOCs.* Reich & Ruipérez-Valiente 2019, Science. https://www.science.org/doi/10.1126/science.aav7958
- **Isolation and slow feedback are named dropout factors.** Self-reported. *Thin.* Frontiers in Education 2024. https://www.frontiersin.org/journals/education/articles/10.3389/feduc.2024.1345205/full

## Asking for help

- **Students avoid asking to avoid looking stupid; big classes make it worse.** Interviews, n=19. *Thin but consistent.* Severe et al. 2024, PLOS ONE. https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC10846701/
- **Seeking understanding helps; seeking just the answer hurts.** 108 studies, n=37,941: instrumental help-seeking correlates positively with achievement, executive (answer-getting) negatively. *Solid, correlational.* Fong et al. 2023, Journal of Educational Psychology. https://www.ovid.com/journals/jedup/abstract/10.1037/edu0000725~academic-help-seeking-and-achievement-of-postsecondary

## What works

- **Bloom's "2 sigma" is not a real number.** Two 1980s dissertations, author-written tests; about half the effect was mastery testing, not tutoring. Randomized tutoring trials pool at ~0.29-0.37 SD. *Folklore as usually cited.* https://www.educationnext.org/two-sigma-tutoring-separating-science-fiction-from-science-fact/ and Nickow, Oreopoulos & Quan 2024, AERJ https://journals.sagepub.com/doi/abs/10.3102/00028312231208687
- **Step-by-step tutoring nearly matches a human; answer-only tutoring is half as good.** Human 0.79 SD, step-based software 0.76, answer-only ~0.40. *Solid direction.* VanLehn 2011. https://www.semanticscholar.org/paper/3b924661db089b3511465ae48e6400e20a1dd232
- **Testing yourself beats rereading.** g = 0.50 (Rowland 2014, Psychological Bulletin, https://www.researchgate.net/publication/264988491); g = 0.61 across 217 studies, classroom effects similar to lab (Adesope et al. 2017, https://journals.sagepub.com/doi/abs/10.3102/0034654316689306). *Solid.*
- **Spacing study out beats cramming;** the best gap grows with how long you need to remember. 317 experiments. *Solid; specific "optimal interval" algorithms are thin.* Cepeda et al. 2006, Psychological Bulletin. https://augmentingcognition.com/assets/Cepeda2006.pdf
- **Explained feedback beats right/wrong feedback.** Elaborated d = 0.49, correct answer only 0.32, right/wrong only 0.05. *Solid.* Van der Kleij et al. 2015, Review of Educational Research. https://journals.sagepub.com/doi/abs/10.3102/0034654314564881
- **Worked examples help beginners and hurt experts** (expertise reversal): support should fade as you improve. *Solid in well-structured domains.* Kalyuga et al. https://link.springer.com/article/10.1007/s10648-007-9054-3
- **Naming and refuting the specific wrong idea beats re-explaining the right one.** g = 0.41 across 44 comparisons; a second meta-analysis of 76 studies agrees. *Solid.* Schroeder & Kucera 2022 https://link.springer.com/article/10.1007/s10648-021-09656-z and Danielson et al. 2025 https://www.tandfonline.com/doi/full/10.1080/00461520.2024.2365628

## AI tutors and study tools

- **When Khanmigo refused to give answers, most students stopped using it,** and it didn't improve math scores over Khan Academy practice alone. Two years, 18 Tennessee middle schools. Hechinger Report, Sep 2026. https://hechingerreport.org/proof-points-khanmigo-math-ai-tutor/
- **ChatGPT Study Mode "kept implying that I had mastered the material when I clearly had not."** Edutopia hands-on test, Feb 2026. https://www.edutopia.org/article/putting-chatgpts-study-mode-through-its-paces/
